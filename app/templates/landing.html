Install Recruit as an app for instant notifications

Recruit works in any browser, but to get push notifications on your phone you need to add it to your home screen. It takes ~15 seconds.

⬇️

Android / Chrome

  1. Open recruit.98487840.xyz in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right)
  3. Tap “Add to Home screen” / “Install app”
  4. Confirm — an app icon appears on your home screen
🍎

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open recruit.98487840.xyz in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button (the box-with-up-arrow)
  3. Scroll and tap “Add to Home Screen”
  4. Tap Add — it opens full-screen like a real app
💻

Desktop (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Open recruit.98487840.xyz
  2. Click the install icon (⊞ / ⬆) in the address bar, or the top-right ⋮→ “Install Recruit”
  3. It launches in its own window, ready for notifications
🔔 After installing, open the app and tap “Enable Notifications” so pushes reach you.
